Our Cabinet Painting Process

1. Consultation

We start with a detailed consultation to understand your color preferences and finish goals.

2. Color & Finish Selection

We help you choose the right color and finish to complement your space.

3. Surface Preparation

We clean, sand, and prime every surface so the paint bonds correctly and lasts.

4. Painting

Our painters apply multiple coats for a smooth, even, factory-quality finish.

5. Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough inspection to ensure the finish meets our high standards.
